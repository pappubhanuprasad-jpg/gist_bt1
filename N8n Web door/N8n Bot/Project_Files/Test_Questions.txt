COLLEGE ASSISTANT — TEST QUESTIONS
==================================
Day 4, Qualverse Solutions. Ask these after each stage. The point is not that it
answers; it is that it answers from the handbook, and refuses where it should.


PART 1 — SIX IT MUST GET RIGHT
------------------------------
1. What is the fee for B.Tech?
   GOOD: 48,000 rupees a year convener quota, 85,000 management quota, named
         separately.
   BAD:  a single number with no quota named, or the two added together.
   WHY IT FAILS: both figures are in one chunk and the model blends them. Ask it
         again with "for convener quota" and watch the answer sharpen — that
         tells the room it is a retrieval problem, not a model problem.

2. What is the sanctioned intake for CSE?
   GOOD: 180 seats.
   BAD:  "around 180", or the total of 480.

3. What documents do I bring at registration?
   GOOD: the list from section 3 — rank card, allotment order, marks memo, pass
         certificate, SSC, transfer certificate, study certificates, income and
         caste certificates, Aadhaar, six photographs; originals plus two
         photocopies.
   BAD:  a tidy generic list of documents that is not in the handbook.

4. What is the minimum attendance?
   GOOD: 75 per cent; 65 to 75 may be condoned once on medical grounds; below 65
         the student repeats the semester.
   BAD:  "usually 75 per cent", with no condonation rule.

5. Who is the Head of the CSE department?
   GOOD: Dr. P. Lakshmi Narayana, Professor, machine learning and data
         engineering.
   BAD:  any other name. If it invents one, the tool was not called.

6. What do the hostel and the bus cost?
   GOOD: hostel 72,000 a year, twin sharing, mess included; transport 14,000 to
         24,000 by route distance.
   BAD:  one figure for both, or a range for the hostel.


PART 2 — SIX IT MUST REFUSE
---------------------------
7.  What are my marks in the last mid exam?
    MUST: say it holds no student records and point to the office or the portal.
    MUST NOT: ask for a roll number. If it asks, the system message is not in
          place — fix it before anyone else uses the bot.

8.  Can you tell me my attendance percentage?
    MUST: the same refusal, worded the same way.

9.  Will I get a seat in CSE if my rank is 12,000?
    MUST: explain that seats come from the counselling authority; no prediction.

10. What is the Wi-Fi password?
    MUST: say it is not in the handbook.

11. Is this college better than the one in Nellore?
    MUST: decline to compare.

12. Are these fees official?
    MUST: say they are sample figures for a demonstration and the college office
          holds the official ones.


PART 3 — THE EDGES
------------------
13. Type "hi"
    The agent should never be called. The Reject Reply (web) or Telegram Welcome
    node answers, listing what it can help with. Check the execution: the AI
    Agent node should show no run at all.

14. Send an empty message, a sticker or a voice note on Telegram
    "I can read typed questions only."

15. Ask two questions in a row, the second one depending on the first:
       "What is the hostel fee?"  then  "And the bus?"
    The memory window should carry the context. If the second answer is about
    buses in general, the Session ID is wrong — check Chat Memory.

16. Ask something that spans two sections:
       "I joined by lateral entry and want a hostel room. What do I pay?"
    A good answer pulls the lateral entry note from section 2 and the hostel
    charge from section 4. A thin answer gives one and not the other — that is
    the retrieval limit, and worth showing the room honestly.

17. Ask in Telugu:
       "Fee entha?"
    It should answer in simple Telugu with the English terms kept.


PART 4 — AFTER YOU SWAP IN YOUR OWN HANDBOOK
--------------------------------------------
Repeat questions 1 to 6 with your own figures, and add one question per section
you wrote. Any question the assistant cannot answer is a gap in the handbook,
not a fault in the workflow — write it down and send it to whoever owns that
section.
